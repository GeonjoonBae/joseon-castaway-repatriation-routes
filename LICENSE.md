# Reuse conditions

No single licence applies to every file in this repository.

The reconstructed route data and figure-specific annotations are pre-publication research outputs. No permission for republication, redistribution, or creation of derivative datasets is granted at this stage.

Third-party data remain subject to their original terms:

- CHGIS-derived boundaries, lakes, coordinates, and background tiles remain subject to CHGIS terms and attribution requirements.
- Natural Earth components remain subject to the Natural Earth terms of use.
- Leaflet is loaded from its public distribution and remains subject to the Leaflet licence.

The maps may be viewed for scholarly peer review. A formal public-data licence can be added after publication and rights review.
